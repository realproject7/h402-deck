02THE ARC THESIS

Payments are solved. Trusted autonomous choice is not.

Existing rails can move USDC. h402 adds the record that tells an agent, a provider, and a future evaluator why a provider was selected and whether the paid result was useful.

“Why this provider, for this task, under this budget?”

For autonomous commerce, the answer cannot disappear with the request. It needs an attributable decision, a final settlement reference, and an outcome signal.

01 · POLICY

Bound the spend

Set the task scope, provider eligibility, and USDC ceiling before execution.

02 · RECEIPT

Explain the choice

Anchor the selection criteria, quote, and payment reference as a signed receipt.

03 · OUTCOME

Verify the result

Attach an evaluator reference to a result hash without exposing private payloads.

04 · REPUTATION

Improve future routing

Use verified outcomes to make provider performance portable and reusable.
