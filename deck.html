05The receipt

Keep the information that makes a payment explainable.

The onchain record does not duplicate API output. It anchors the decision context and a tamper-evident reference to the observed result.

  • Selection policy and USDC limit make autonomous spend reviewable.
  • Provider, quote, and payment reference make the settlement traceable.
  • Outcome hash lets an evaluator attach quality feedback without exposing private payloads.
