The team bringing the x402 payment rail — and the Agentic Commerce trend it started — to GIWA. ##Core strengths

The team that can actually bring Agentic Commerce to GIWA

x402 turned HTTP 402 into a live, agent-native payment standard — and we already build on it in production. h402 implements the full x402 handshake (402 quote → local EIP-3009 signing → facilitator settlement, non-custodial, no API keys). Porting this rail to GIWA — including operating GIWA's x402 facilitator — is an execution task for this team, not research.

A product-level router, waiting for a rail — a new market for GIWA

h402 is already at product completeness: 166 capabilities across 75 providers on the live catalog, every route verified with real paid calls, best-provider routing by live quality-adjusted price. Once the payment rail lands on GIWA, hundreds-to-thousands of agent-callable APIs form a new pay-per-call market settling on GIWA from day one — transaction volume, not promises.

Lean team, all three already shipping AI × Web3

Three people — engineer, designer, marketer — and every one of us has founded and operated multiple AI × Web3 products (see the personal-projects lists and the 19-entry track record below). Deep ecosystem literacy on both sides: onchain economics and agent tooling.

##What we're building on GIWA

Two deliverables: (1) h402 adds GIWA as a settlement chain — every capability in the catalog becomes payable in stablecoins on GIWA, per call; (2) we build and operate the GIWA x402 facilitator — the service that verifies signed payment payloads and settles them onchain, so any seller on GIWA can accept agent payments without touching a node. GIWA's 1-second blocks and low fees are exactly the environment agent micropayments need.

One router call for the agent — quote, signature, and onchain settlement handled underneath.
